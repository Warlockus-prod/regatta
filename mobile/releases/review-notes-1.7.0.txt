Week to Regatta is a sailing learning and practice app. No sign-in or test account is required.

Version 1.7.0 (50) brings a new design and structured lessons: Home continues the learner's course, a Menu tab lists every section, "Sailing from scratch" has 8 lessons in three modules and "Working with sails" has 13 lessons in four modules (including a new winch and clutch lesson with an interactive line bench). A lesson's practice step opens the matching trainer, which starts on the lesson task and offers a button back to the lesson.

The trainers (Practice tab) keep the boat on screen with a tabbed control dock below it. An optional wheel mode (gear button in the trainer, or Settings > Trainers) lets the learner steer and trim with on-screen wheels; wheel clicks are a sound effect that plays only when the learner turns sound on in that setting. The trainer screens may rotate to landscape; the rest of the app stays portrait.

No new permissions, no new data collection: the app still collects only anonymous usage statistics (not linked to identity, no tracking), which can be turned off in Settings.

Verified on simulators with iOS 27.0 (iPhone 18 Pro) and iOS 26.5 (iPhone 17 Pro): fresh install of the Release build and launch, and installation over version 1.6.4 with the learner's progress kept.
