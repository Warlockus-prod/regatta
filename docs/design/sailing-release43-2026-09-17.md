## Limits

Ten planned lessons, full equipment state machines, partial hoists/reefing,
calibrated loads and instructor validation remain outside this release.
Outhaul changes modeled depth, not clew position or a full mechanical line path.
The single saved checkpoint is manual, not autosave or cross-device sync.
Offline here describes the native bundled features, not first-visit website
access without internet. Speech assessment, AI, weather, multiplayer and other
online services still require connectivity. Physical-iPhone thermal, gesture
and memory testing has not been completed.
