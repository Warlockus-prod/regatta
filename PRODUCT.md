# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary users are candidates preparing for the Polish UKE SRC examination, including Russian-speaking learners who need additional explanations. They study on a phone or laptop, often in short evening sessions, and later use the same material as an onboard refresher.

Their main job is to understand why marine radio procedures work as they do, connect that theory to official exam questions, and then perform the procedure correctly under pressure in a simulator or aboard a vessel.

## Product Purpose

Week to Regatta turns a compressed exam bank into a coherent learning path from mental model to procedure to practice. Success means that a learner can choose the correct communication priority, operate a VHF DSC radio, send and receive calls safely, explain the reason for each step, pass the UKE theory exam, and adapt the global rules to local European or non-European practice.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, practical. The product should feel like a trusted instructor on the bridge: clear enough for a beginner, rigorous enough for an experienced sailor, and composed during safety-critical topics.

## Anti-references

- A dense fact dump that assumes unexplained abbreviations are already understood.
- Decorative nautical styling that competes with procedures or safety information.
- Memorising exam tricks without explaining the operational reason.
- Treating regional practice as if it were a universal rule.
- Diagrams that look impressive but cannot be used to make a decision.
- Long uninterrupted pages without progress, hierarchy, or a clear next action.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the reason before asking for the procedure.
2. Use diagrams as working mental models, not decoration.
3. Keep exam truth, operational truth, and regional practice visibly distinct.
4. Close every theory loop with a relevant question, drill, or simulator action.
5. Make safety-critical decisions calm, explicit, and traceable to an authoritative source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operation. Never communicate priority or correctness by color alone. Keep controls at least 44 pixels high, provide text alternatives for diagrams, support reduced motion, preserve readable SVG labels at mobile widths, and keep Polish primary text with optional Russian explanation.
